## Agadir Airport to Taghazout: Transfer Guide for Surfers & Beach Lovers
Just Luxury Transfers·20 June 2026
Heading from Agadir Airport to the surf village of Taghazout? It’s a quick 25-minute coastal hop — here’s the price, the journey, what to do about surfboards, and how to get picked up at arrivals.
Taghazout is Morocco’s laid-back surf capital — 18 km up the coast from Agadir Airport and the launch pad for Taghazout Bay, Anchor Point and a string of golden beaches. The easiest way to start the trip is a private transfer straight from arrivals to your surf camp, riad or villa.
## How far is Taghazout from Agadir Airport?
About 18 km — a scenic 25-minute drive along the coast road. Our driver meets you inside the terminal with a name sign and takes you directly to your accommodation in Taghazout or Taghazout Bay.
## How much does it cost?
A private Agadir Airport → Taghazout transfer starts at €35 for a sedan (up to 3 passengers), fixed and per vehicle. SUVs and vans are available for groups, extra luggage or multiple boards.
## Travelling with surfboards?
No problem — standard boards travel free in our SUVs and vans. If you’ve got several boards or bulky bags, just pick a larger vehicle or mention it when you book and we’ll make sure everything fits comfortably.
## Where we drop you off
Anywhere in Taghazout, Taghazout Bay, Tamraght or Aourir (Banana Village) — surf camps, hotels, riads and private villas included. Door-to-door, so you’re not lugging boards from a taxi rank.
## Better than a grand taxi
Shared grand taxis run the route cheaply but leave when full, squeeze in strangers, and rarely take boards happily. A pre-booked transfer is private, fixed-price, waiting when you land, and sized to your crew and kit.
