Just Luxury Transfers·20 June 2026
A private transfer is the easiest way out of Agadir Al Massira Airport (AGA) — fixed prices, a driver waiting at arrivals, and flight tracking. Here are the fares to every popular destination and how to book in two minutes.
Landing at Agadir Al Massira Airport (AGA) and not sure how to reach your hotel? A private airport transfer is the simplest, most predictable option: your driver waits in arrivals with a name sign, helps with the bags, and takes you straight to the door for a price agreed in advance — no meter, no haggling, no shared minibus.
## How far is Agadir Airport from the city?
Agadir Al Massira Airport sits about 25 km east of the city centre — roughly a 30-minute drive depending on traffic and your exact hotel. Taghazout and the northern beaches are a little further up the coast (~25–40 minutes).

## Private transfer vs. airport taxi
Petit and grand taxis are available outside arrivals, but the price is negotiated on the spot and rarely fixed for tourists. A pre-booked private transfer locks the fare before you fly, guarantees a clean air-conditioned vehicle sized to your group, and means someone is already waiting when you land — even if the flight is delayed.
