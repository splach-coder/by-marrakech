## Agadir to Essaouira: Day Trip & Private Transfer Guide
Just Luxury Transfers·20 June 2026
The windswept, walled port of Essaouira is one of the best day trips from Agadir. Here’s the drive, what to see, whether to do it as a tour or a one-way transfer, and what it costs.
Essaouira — the breezy, blue-and-white walled city on the Atlantic — is one of the most rewarding day trips from Agadir. Think wave-battered ramparts, a working fishing port, art galleries in the medina, and the famously fresh grilled seafood. It’s close enough to see in a day, or a great one-way stop if you’re moving north.
## How far is Essaouira from Agadir?
Around 175 km up the coast — about 2 hours 15 minutes by private car through the argan-tree country the region is famous for. The drive itself is part of the experience.
## What to see in Essaouira
The UNESCO-listed medina and its blue-shuttered lanes
The Skala de la Ville ramparts and cannons over the Atlantic
The bustling fishing port and fresh-seafood grills
Argan oil women’s cooperatives on the route
Wide windy beach — a world capital of kitesurfing
## Day trip vs. one-way transfer
Two easy ways to do it. Join a guided Essaouira day trip from Agadir (from €40 per person, hotel pickup included) to see the highlights and return the same evening. Or, if you’re continuing your travels, book a private Agadir → Essaouira transfer (from €120 per vehicle) and we’ll drop you at your riad in the medina.
## The argan cooperative stop
On request we’ll pause at a women’s argan-oil cooperative along the way — a quick, no-pressure look at how Morocco’s “liquid gold” is made, and a nice spot to pick up the real thing.
