## The Best Time to Visit Paradise Valley
Just Luxury Transfers·29 April 2026
When to go to Paradise Valley for the fullest pools and fewest crowds, plus what to bring for a perfect day in the High Atlas.
Paradise Valley, a palm-fringed gorge in the High Atlas about 90 minutes from Agadir, is famous for its turquoise rock pools. Timing your visit makes a big difference.
## Best season
Spring (March–May) is ideal: snowmelt and winter rain keep the pools full and the palms lush. Autumn is also pleasant. By late summer some pools can be low after the dry season.
## Best time of day
Arrive in the morning to enjoy the pools before the midday crowds and heat, especially on weekends.
## What to bring
Swimwear, a towel and water shoes (rocks can be slippery).
Sun protection and plenty of water.
Cash for the small café/parking and any local guides.
