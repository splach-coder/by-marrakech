## Getting Around Agadir: Transfers, Taxis & Transport Explained
Just Luxury Transfers·7 May 2026
Airport transfers, petit and grand taxis, buses and private drivers — a simple guide to getting around Agadir without the stress.
Agadir is easy to navigate once you know your options. Here is how locals and savvy travellers get around.
## From Agadir Airport (AGA)
Agadir Al Massira Airport is about 25 km from the city. The simplest, fixed-price option is a private transfer that meets you at arrivals — see airport transfer prices. Avoid unmetered airport taxis that quote on the spot.
## Petit taxis
Small orange petit taxis are for short trips within the city. They are metered — insist on the meter (“compteur”) — and cannot leave the city limits.
## Grand taxis
Older shared Mercedes that run fixed routes between towns. Cheap but cramped, and they leave when full.
## Buses
Local Alsa buses cover the city cheaply; CTM and Supratours coaches connect Agadir to other cities.
## Private driver
For day trips, intercity routes or stress-free travel, a private driver is the most comfortable choice — fixed prices, your own schedule and door-to-door service. Explore our routes or excursions.
## Frequently Asked Questions
## How much is a taxi from Agadir Airport to the city?
expand_less A fixed-price private transfer starts at about €25 for a sedan. Unmetered airport taxis vary, so agree the price before you get in — or pre-book to avoid surprises.
## Are petit taxis metered?
expand_more Yes, petit taxis are metered for trips within Agadir. Ask the driver to use the meter (“compteur”).
