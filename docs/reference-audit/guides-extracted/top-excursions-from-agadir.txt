## Top 10 Excursions From Agadir
Just Luxury Transfers·14 May 2026
From the turquoise pools of Paradise Valley to the Sahara’s edge and the Red City of Marrakech — the ten best day trips and excursions from Agadir.
Agadir is the perfect base for exploring southern Morocco. Here are ten of our most popular excursions, all with hotel pickup, a professional driver and a private or small-group option.

Swim in turquoise High Atlas rock pools on this easy full-day trip. From €25/person — see the Paradise Valley excursion.
## 2. Marrakech day trip
