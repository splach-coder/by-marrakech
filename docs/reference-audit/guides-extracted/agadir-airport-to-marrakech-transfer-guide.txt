## Agadir Airport to Marrakech: The Complete Transfer Guide
Just Luxury Transfers·20 May 2026
Everything you need to know about getting from Agadir to Marrakech — distance, journey time, your transport options and what a private transfer costs.
Marrakech is one of the most popular trips from Agadir, but there is no airport-to-airport flight and no direct train. This guide covers every way to make the 245 km journey and explains why a private transfer is the most comfortable option for most travellers.
## How far is Marrakech from Agadir?
Marrakech sits about 245 km north-east of Agadir. By road it is roughly a 2 hour 45 minute drive on the modern A7 motorway — longer if you take the scenic Tizi n’Test mountain pass.
## Your options
Private transfer — door to door, fixed price, your own schedule. About 2h 45m with a professional driver.
Coach (CTM / Supratours) — cheapest, but fixed timetables, luggage limits and a station-to-station journey of 3.5–4 hours.
Grand taxi — shared older Mercedes; cheap but cramped and you wait for it to fill up.
There is no passenger train between Agadir and Marrakech, though a high-speed line is planned.

## Tips for the journey
Travel early to beat the heat and arrive in time to explore the medina.
Ask your driver for a comfort stop — it is included.
Travelling as a family or group? One vehicle is far better value than separate bus tickets.
